OLED Night is on

Every site you open is now true black. Text stays readable and photos keep their colors.

Pin it to your toolbar

Click the puzzle-piece icon next to the address bar, then the pin beside OLED Night. Its popup controls the current site.

Choose a mode for any site

Automatic
Recolors light sites. On sites that are already dark, it only deepens their grays.
Full recolor
Always recolors, even when a site already looks dark.
Deepen blacks only
Only pushes the darkest grays to true black.
Invert
For canvas apps such as spreadsheets: flips the page, then flips images back.

Keyboard shortcuts

  • Alt+Shift+E On or off everywhere
  • Alt+Shift+D On or off for the current site
