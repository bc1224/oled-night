Chasing the northern lights on a winter night in Tromsø

By Ana Lindqvist · 6 min read

We left the city lights behind just after nine, driving east until the road ran out and the snow turned blue under a thin moon. The forecast promised clear skies for two hours, maybe three.

"Look north and wait. When it comes, it comes all at once."

At first it was only a pale arc above the fjord. Then the arc began to ripple, green turning to violet at its edges, and for twenty minutes nobody said a word.

Photographers on the shoreline swapped settings in whispers: wide lens, long exposure, a steady tripod and patience.
