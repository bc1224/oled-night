# OLED Night privacy policy

_Last updated: September 19, 2026_

OLED Night is a browser extension that makes web pages dark. It is built to work entirely inside your browser.

**What it collects:** nothing. OLED Night has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It never sends any data off your device.

**What it stores:** your settings (on/off, appearance, brightness, contrast, schedule and per-site choices). They are kept in Chrome's own sync storage, so Chrome can sync them between your signed-in browsers. The developer cannot see them.

**Page content:** to recolor a page, OLED Night reads the colors of the elements on it, inside your browser. It does not save or transmit page content.

**Diagnostic reports:** if you click "Report a broken site", the extension saves a small file to your own computer. It contains the site's address, the extension's settings, and short text snippets (30 characters or fewer) from any text it found hard to read. The file goes nowhere unless you choose to send it to someone.

**Permissions:** OLED Night runs on every site because recoloring pages is its purpose. The `storage` permission saves your settings, `activeTab` identifies the current site for the popup and keyboard shortcut, and `scripting` is used only if you turn on the experimental "reach inside locked web components" setting.

**Changes:** if this policy ever changes, the new version will be published with the extension update.

**Contact:** questions about this policy can be sent to the developer through the Chrome Web Store listing.
